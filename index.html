Why independent

No factory.
No bias.

1
No production line to fill

Taste Right doesn't own a canning line or a bottling hall, so every recommendation is made for your brand and not to fill somebody's next production slot.

2
One relationship, whole picture

You get a single expert who knows your product's history, not a fresh account manager every time you're passed to a new supplier.

3
Matched, not sold to

Partner introductions are based on what your product needs to succeed (volume, format, budget, timeline), not who's easiest to refer.
